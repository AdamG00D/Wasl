Why Ganbak?

📡

No internet needed

Bluetooth, Wi-Fi / hotspot, or both at once — your phone becomes a bridge between them.

🕸️

Messages hop

Every phone relays to the next (up to 6), so a message reaches far beyond your own range.

🔒

Encrypted private chats

End-to-end encryption by default; relays can’t read anything or tell who talks to whom. Safety codes to verify each other.

✍️

Nobody speaks for you

Your name and messages are signed with your key, and Ganbak warns you when someone shows up with a name you know but isn’t them.

📶

Ganbak network

No router? Your phone makes its own Wi-Fi and people nearby join with one tap or a QR code.

⏳

Messages wait

If the person isn’t around, phones nearby carry your sealed message until they show up.

🆘

SOS

Ask everyone running Ganbak around you for help with one tap.

🎮

Games in chats

Tic-tac-toe, battleships, memory, who’s the spy?, quiz, mafia and more.

👥

Groups

Open, password, join-request or invite-only secret groups, with an owner and admins.

What it looks like

Your friend doesn’t have it?

Settings → “Share Ganbak”: your phone opens a network, your friend scans two codes and downloads the app straight from your phone — no internet.

FAQ

Does Ganbak need the internet?

No. It works over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi between nearby phones, with no internet, no data plan and no server.

Is there a chat app that works without internet?

Yes — Ganbak is an offline chat app for Android that sends messages, photos and voice notes over Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or a hotspot, with no internet at all.

Are there games that work without internet?

Yes — Ganbak has 9 offline multiplayer games: tic-tac-toe, four in a row, battleships, rock paper scissors, dots and boxes, memory, who’s the spy?, quiz and mafia — over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.

Can I send photos and voice notes without internet?

Yes: photos, videos, voice notes and files, plus voice and video calls over Wi-Fi or a hotspot — all with no internet.

Can I use it on a plane, in the desert or at Hajj?

Yes, anywhere with no signal. On a plane, turn Bluetooth or Wi-Fi on in airplane mode (if the flight allows it).

Can I chat over Bluetooth only?

Yes. Pick Bluetooth on the first screen and nearby phones find each other; with Wi-Fi or a hotspot around, Ganbak can use both at once.

How do I reach someone far away?

Messages hop through other phones running Ganbak (up to 6). And if they’re not around right now, your message waits and reaches them when they show up.

Is it encrypted?

Private chats are end-to-end encrypted automatically. Details and known limits are written plainly in the security page on GitHub.

Is Ganbak free?

Yes — free and open source (GPL-3.0), with no ads and no analytics.

Which phones?

Android 7 or newer. Starting a Ganbak network needs Android 8+, joining one from inside the app Android 10+.
