| `Wasl-1.1-arm64-v8a.apk` | **Most phones** (2017 and newer) — pick this if unsure |
| `Wasl-1.1-armeabi-v7a.apk` | Older or low-end phones |
| `Wasl-1.1-x86_64.apk` | Emulators and Chromebooks |
| `Wasl-1.1-universal.apk` | Any device (larger download) |

All variants share the same version code, so you can update from any of them to any other.
Android 7.0+. Verify with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## What's new

- **Bridge mode** — run Wi-Fi and Bluetooth together so your phone links a Wi-Fi/hotspot network with people on
  Bluetooth, turning several networks into one mesh.
- **Group administration** — the owner appoints admins; admins remove members, delete their messages for everyone
  and rename the group. Nobody can act on the owner. Admin commands are authenticated per recipient.
- **Chat-style notifications** — sender, avatar and text, grouped per conversation, with *Reply* and *Mark as read*.
  With the app lock on they only say "new message"; the phone's lock screen never shows content.
- **Mute** any chat or group (1 h / 8 h / 1 week / always) and **pin** chats to the top.
- **Cartoon avatars** (24, drawn in-app) shown next to each member's messages in groups; tap one for private
  chat, call, mention, block and admin actions.
- **Groups**: "typing…", read receipts (blue ticks when everyone connected has read) and *Message info* showing who read.
- **Mentions** with `@` — highlighted, and they notify even in muted groups.
- **Reactions**, **forwarding** and **search** inside a conversation.
- **Home**: swipe between pages; joined groups appear in the chats list; *Nearby* lists people and groups.
- The project is now **open source** (GPL-3.0).

## Fixes

  top of another.

- Join-request rejections are authenticated.

## Notes

- Removing a member re-keys **join-request** groups (a real cryptographic removal). In **password** and **open**
  groups it is a ban enforced by the apps — see [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/AdamG00D/Wasl/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
- Both sides need 1.1 for reactions, mentions and admin commands; 1.0 still works for chats and groups.
